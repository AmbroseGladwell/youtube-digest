# Dubious reasons

OV-85. The verdict's `dubious` flag used to be one bare bit, and about a quarter of the
first 36 notes carried it with nothing to say why. A reader who can't see the reason
ignores the flag or distrusts it. Now the flag is a button: pressing it opens a short
explanation under it, with one item per claim. Each item names the claim, says what is wrong with
it, and links to the moment in the video where it is said.

**What is built, and where:**

|---|---|
| The shape: claim, basis, reason, `startMs`; at most three | `packages/domain`: `DubiousClaim`, `DubiousBasis`, `Verdict.dubiousClaims` |
| The flag follows from the claims, so the two can never disagree | `Verdict`'s refinement; `assembleOverview.ts` |

## The model lists claims, and code sets the flag

The prompt no longer asks for a `dubious` boolean. It asks for `dubiousClaims`, which is
empty for most videos, and code sets `dubious` to whether that list is empty. The bar for
listing a claim is the same one the flag had (`overview-generation-decisions.md`,
"Soundness collapses to one bit"). It is a claim that contradicts something settled, or
one tied to a conflict of interest. Contested, unresolved or merely unfamiliar claims do
not count. Deriving the flag from the list means no note can be flagged without a reason,
or carry a reason without the flag.

**Basis is one of two values**, `contradictsSettled` or `conflictOfInterest`, matching
the bar. The card suggested more, such as self-reported figures, no evidence given, or a
sales pitch. Those are weak reasoning, not dubiousness, and they stay in the reasoning
prose. Adding them as bases would have lowered the bar, so the flag would fire more often.

**At most three, the worst first.** The flag is meant to be rare, and a fourth item is
noise.

## The model names a segment, never a time

This is the same device chapters and the watch-it-anyway range use
(`docs/prototype/constraints.md`). For each claim the model gives `segmentIndex` into the
numbered transcript, and `startMs` is that segment's start. An index past the
transcript is a `GenerationError`, not a clamp. With no transcript the index can only be
null, and the claim is shown with no link.

The prompt asks for the claim "as close to the transcript as you can", in 25 words, and the
reason in 30. Both are enforced at 30 and 40, so a near miss doesn't fail the overview.

## Notes from before this existed

Migration 6 gives every older verdict `dubiousClaims: null`. Nothing in an older record
says why, so null is the honest value, and it differs from `[]`. The panel says
"No reason was saved with this overview" rather than showing an empty list, and the markdown
says the same. With one reader, the existing dubious notes are regenerated by hand
(delete and capture again) rather than backfilled.

## The panel

Designed in Claude Design as `OV-85 Dubious Reasons.dc.html` (frames 85a–85g).

**Inline, not a modal.** The flag is a disclosure button, with `aria-expanded` and
`aria-controls`, in the masthead's byline. It opens a raised card between the byline and
the title, and the card pushes the note down. There is no scrim and nothing covers the note.
In the side panel the video stays in view while a claim's moment moves it. Opening moves
focus to the heading, "Why it’s flagged". Escape, the ✕ or the flag itself closes the
panel, and focus returns to the flag. With no backdrop there is no focus trap. The panel's
height opens over 200ms where the browser can animate to `auto`; elsewhere, and with
reduced motion, it simply appears.

**The flag** is warning-coloured text with the alert icon and a chevron. It reads "Dubious
claim", or "2 dubious claims" when there is more than one, and the count also sits under
the heading ("2 claims, worst first"). Hover and pressed tint it from the warning colour.
While open it keeps the tint, gains a warning ring, and its chevron turns up. Below 480px
(phone, side panel) it drops the words but keeps them as its accessible name. It keeps the
icon, the count and the chevron, so channel, verdict and flag stay on one line, and the
channel truncates first.

**Each item** is the basis in the warning colour, the claim in quotes, the reason in muted
text, and the moment as a surface pill. That moment does what the watch-it-anyway jump does.
Beside the video it is "Skip to 6:52": it moves the player and leaves the panel open, so
the next claim is one press away. Everywhere else it is "Watch from 6:52", a link to
YouTube at that second. A claim with no time ends after its reason.

**Close is an ✕ on the surface fill.** Action orange stays for making things
(`stone-theme.md`). Below 480px the ✕, the moment pills and "This looks wrong" are 44px
tall.

**"This looks wrong" is a stand-in for OV-86.** The foot is its own row so that card's
feedback sheet can replace it without moving anything else. Until then the button records
`reader.dubiousReasons.markedWrong`, and the foot becomes "Thanks, noted." with a tick,
announced through a polite live region, with focus kept there.

The library card's chip stays a label. The card as a whole links to the reader, and the
explanation lives there.

## Counted

`reader.dubiousReasons.opened({ reasonsSaved })`, where `0` means a note from before
reasons were saved, since a dubious verdict with an empty list cannot exist.
`reader.dubiousReasons.momentFollowed({ by })` and `reader.dubiousReasons.markedWrong()`.
